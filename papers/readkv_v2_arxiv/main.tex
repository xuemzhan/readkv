\begin{abstract}
Cross-model key--value (KV) handoff promises zero re-prefill of a source
document. We report a negative, empirical data-audit study on a paired
synthetic benchmark. An earlier release claimed a utility-preserving
counterfactual gap and a receiver-side Value-consumption mechanism. A
data-quality audit overturned both: 18 degenerate pairs, $\geq15$ groups of
verbatim cross-split leakage, complete train/test template overlap, and a
pair-level i.i.d.\ bootstrap. After repair the result reverses. On a single
synthetic equal-depth MVP pair (1.7B$\to$0.6B), the affine mapper collapses
dual-side correctness from $\approx0.82$ to $0.28$--$0.42$ across three seeds;
$E_{\mathrm{CF}}$ rises to $6.90$--$8.54$, approaching but not exceeding
mismatched ($8.38$) and zero-cache ($8.58$) controls. \texttt{transfer\_broken}
is set by a single-signal dual-drop, marking a failure boundary. A strong
\texttt{kd\_only} baseline preserves utility (dual $0.70$--$0.77$) yet retains
$E_{\mathrm{CF}}$ $6.39$--$7.21$, reported only as exploratory. The comparison
is budget-asymmetric: affine has zero trainable parameters and no model
training, \texttt{kd\_only} 7.34M parameters and 8 training epochs. All clean
CIs are exploratory (8 \texttt{template\_family} clusters, $<24$), from one
synthetic MVP pair; the sealed test remains unevaluated. Contributions~(1)
and~(2) are withdrawn and~(3) withheld: data defects manufactured a false
finding. No method was trained, the primary endpoint is unevaluated, and no
improvement is claimed.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Cross-model KV handoff maps a teacher's document cache into a receiver
student's cache format so that the student can answer queries about a document
without re-processing it (\emph{strict zero re-prefill}). An earlier version of
this study asked whether the student's \emph{functional dependence on the
document} survives transfer, formalized as counterfactual functional fidelity
and measured by the counterfactual error $E_{\mathrm{CF}}$
(Section~\ref{sec:metrics}). It reported two positive findings on a synthetic
corpus: an apparent gap between ordinary transfer utility and counterfactual
fidelity, and a receiver-side Value-consumption mechanism. \textbf{Both are
withdrawn here.}

The paper is therefore not a method or mechanism paper. Its subject is the
\emph{audit trail} that overturned those findings and the boundary condition
that emerged once the data were repaired. An independent data-quality audit
found that the original corpus contained degenerate pairs (identical candidates
marked must-flip), cross-split verbatim document leakage, and train/test
template-skeleton overlap, and that its cluster bootstrap had degenerated to
pair-level i.i.d. Rebuilding the corpus with these defects removed reversed the
central observation: the previously ``utility-preserving gap'' became a
\emph{transfer failure} (Section~\ref{sec:reversal}). The positive signals were
artifacts of the flawed data, not properties of KV transfer. This is the
methodological lesson of the study; it is consistent with the general
reproducibility warning of \citet{kapoor2023}, but our contribution is the
concrete, preregistered before/after case and the paired counterfactual
$E_{\mathrm{CF}}$ audit trail in this specific benchmark, not the general
lesson itself.

\paragraph{Findings reported here (negative / empirical results, not positive
contributions).}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Failure boundary.} On clean data the tested affine/ridge mapper
      fails to transfer usefully on the single equal-depth MVP pair: dual-side
      correctness collapses to $0.28$--$0.42$ from a native $\approx0.78$--$0.90$,
      and $E_{\mathrm{CF}}$ rises towards but does not reach the
      mismatched/zero-cache controls (Section~\ref{sec:mvp}). This is a
      boundary condition under the present data construction, not a general
      property of KV transfer.
\item \textbf{Exploratory utility-preserving gap on the strong baseline.} The
      \texttt{kd\_only} baseline on the same clean pair preserves transfer
      utility (dual $0.70$--$0.77$, \texttt{transfer\_broken=false}) yet retains
      a stable, nonzero $E_{\mathrm{CF}}$ of $6.39$--$7.21$. We report this
      \emph{only as an exploratory observation} on one synthetic pair, not as a
      restored contribution (Section~\ref{sec:kdbase}).
\item \textbf{Methodological negative result.} A controlled before/after
      comparison shows how three concrete data defects plus a degenerate
      bootstrap produced a statistically convincing but spurious positive
      finding; repairing the data erased and reversed it
      (Section~\ref{sec:reversal}).
\item \textbf{Limited external anchor.} On SQuAD~v2.0 dev the affine failure
      direction replicates (Joint F1 falls to $2.6\%$ of Self) but the kd
      utility-preserving contrast does not, and all arms hit an EM floor; under
      the preregistered v2.4 rule this is \texttt{exploratory\_disclosure}, not
      a strengthening of external validity (Section~\ref{sec:anchor}).
\end{enumerate}
None of these is a positive contribution in the sense of the frozen
contribution criteria. We no longer claim to identify a general counterfactual
gap, to localize a mechanism, or to improve transfer. Contribution~(3) remains
\emph{withheld}, the primary endpoint is unevaluated, P2/P3/P4 are permanently
cancelled, real-document external validity is unverified (\texttt{real=0}), and
the only authorized external probe is evaluation-only SQuAD (Section~\ref{sec:discussion}).

\section{Background and Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Cross-model KV transfer and the utility/fidelity question.}
Behavioral distillation of per-layer linear K/V maps
(\citep{kvlingo}) and functional cache transport with frozen-receiver
supervision (\citep{cachelink}) established that ordinary task utility or
token-distribution matching is the standard target. At the level reported in
their abstracts, \citet{cachelink} states that lower reconstruction error need
not imply higher receiver utility, and \citet{heo2026} report a closed-form
per-head linear mapping with degradation on some model pairs; we do not rely
on finer-grained, section-level details of either work. These works score
ordinary task quality, not paired counterfactual response change; they are
baselines and conceptual precursors here, and the ``partial-pair transfer
failure'' we observe is consistent with the degradation reported by
\citet{heo2026}. We do not treat the utility/fidelity distinction as our
invention.

\paragraph{A crowded 2026 method and audit landscape.}
Recent work has explicitly separated utility from fidelity: a cross-version
study introduces \emph{Functional Cache Divergence} and distinguishes task
utility from fidelity to native behavior~\citep{n1}; a fidelity--consumability
study reports that eight substitute-cache methods remain at the no-information
baseline and that ``more faithful'' need not mean
``consumable''~\citep{n2}; a safety study finds value-reuse bias exceeds key-reuse
bias and applies per-layer/per-head affine
correction~\citep{n3}. Strong transfer baselines and failure analyses---sparse
query-relevant transfer~\citep{n4}, prefill-free cross-family
transfer~\citep{n5}, rank-disagreement selective
recomputation~\citep{n6}, and training-free cache
fusion~\citep{n8}---further compress the methodological space, and hybrid/linear
attention translation is also being explored~\citep{n9a,n9b}. At abstract
level, a causal-audit preprint reports mismatched/zero/random cache controls
and a preregistered equivalence test~\citep{n7}, anticipating our control-group
and information-attribution discipline. These works anticipate both the general
utility-versus-fidelity distinction and the control-group methodology; our
contribution is \emph{not} a general distinction, a mechanism-layer first, or a
new audit method, but a specific preregistered reversal case with a paired
counterfactual $E_{\mathrm{CF}}$ audit trail. Our characterizations of this
2026 preprint literature (\citep{n1,n2,n3,n4,n5,n6,n7,n8,n9a,n9b}) are
abstract-level; we have not verified section-level claims.

\paragraph{Counterfactual and faithfulness evaluation.}
Paired minimal-pair construction that changes the gold label is established by
Contrast Sets~\citep{contrastsets} and counterfactually augmented
data~\citep{kaushik2020}; $E_{\mathrm{CF}}$ is a continuous logit-difference
adaptation of that idea to the transfer setting, not a new evaluation paradigm.
Under KV-cache compression, ``Does Accuracy Equal Evidence?''~\citep{acc_evidence}
is reported (at abstract level) to find that answer accuracy can be preserved
while evidence faithfulness degrades; we distinguish their within-model,
compression, reasoning-chain setting from our cross-model, paired
counterfactual-document setting. Real-document external
validity is not claimed: the AI-generated-data proposal was evaluated and
withdrawn (not activated; see Section~\ref{sec:datafix}). The only standard-QA
anchor we report is SQuAD~v2.0~\citep{squad16,squad18}, used for evaluation
only (Section~\ref{sec:anchor}).

\section{Research Questions}
\label{sec:rq}

Following the frozen scope update, the study is framed by four descriptive,
negative/empirical questions. They carry no positive-contribution threshold and
are not method-paper claims.

\begin{description}
\item[RQ1 (methodological lesson; core).] To what extent did data-quality
      defects manufacture the reported ``utility-preserving gap''? Evidence:
      the defect inventory and repair report (Section~\ref{sec:datafix}) and the
      before/after comparison (Section~\ref{sec:reversal}). Adjustment: if the
      audit trail were incomplete, we would report only the defect list and not
      claim a directional reversal; we do not claim the general leakage lesson
      as new.
\item[RQ2 (failure boundary; core).] On clean data, under which conditions does
      the tested affine mapper fail to transfer usefully? Evidence: three clean
      seeds on the equal-depth pair, dual-side collapse, and the
      \texttt{transfer\_broken} annotation read jointly with the frozen machine
      verdict (Sections~\ref{sec:mvp},~\ref{sec:discussion}). Adjustment: we
      disclose that $E_{\mathrm{CF}}$ approaches but does not exceed the
      mismatch/zero-cache controls and that the break flag rests on one signal.
\item[RQ3 (exploratory observation; not a contribution).] Does the strong
      \texttt{kd\_only} baseline preserve transfer utility while retaining a
      nonzero $E_{\mathrm{CF}}$, and is that gap dissociable from ordinary QA
      quality? Evidence: three clean kd seeds and the SQuAD anchor
      (Sections~\ref{sec:kdbase},~\ref{sec:anchor}). Adjustment: reported as an
      exploratory observation only; not a restoration of any withdrawn claim.
\item[RQ4 (external-validity boundary).] With \texttt{real=0}, synthetic data,
      and a single pair, how far can compliant anchors extend the conclusions?
      Evidence: the evaluation-only SQuAD anchor and the blocked real-document
      attempt (Sections~\ref{sec:datafix},~\ref{sec:anchor}). Adjustment: real=0
      and the single synthetic equal-depth pair are disclosed as limitations;
      the withdrawn data proposal produced no result.
\end{description}

\label{sec:data}

differing in one targeted fact, with candidates A/B held fixed within the pair
and their order randomly swapped. Six slices exclude distinct confounds:

from the flip denominator; F6 is excluded as well under the frozen v2.3
convention. The rebuilt corpus has 520 pairs

Degenerate pairs, cross-split verbatim leakage (including
train$\leftrightarrow$test whole-pair overlap), and pair-level bootstrap
clusters (300 unique pairs but only 8 independent template structures) inflated
flip/dual/$E_{\mathrm{CF}}$ statistics and narrowed confidence intervals. These
defects directly produced the spurious positive findings withdrawn here. The
corpus was rebuilt with forced candidate distinctness, split-disjoint entity
pools, deterministic deduplication, and 16 structurally distinct unseen
templates. Acceptance checks pass 11/11 and a 53-test unit suite passes with no
regressions. Under the frozen statistics convention \texttt{v2.3}, the primary
bootstrap cluster unit is \texttt{template\_family}, with a mandatory pair-level
sensitivity analysis; runs with fewer than 24 families are flagged
\texttt{exploratory}. The flip denominator is $398$ under
excludes $=[\mathrm{F5},\mathrm{F6}]$. The two template counts refer to different
granularities and should not be conflated: the rebuilt corpus has $56$
\emph{corpus-wide} template families (over $24$ basic structures), whereas each
data split---including the validation split used for all results here---contains
only $8$ distinct \texttt{template\_family} clusters. It is the latter, per-split
count that enters the bootstrap ($n_{\mathrm{clusters}}=8<24$), which is why all
clean CIs are flagged \texttt{exploratory} under v2.3.

\paragraph{Real documents: M4 blocked, \texttt{real=0}.} The attempt to collect
real (non-synthetic) counterfactual documents (task M4) was formally recorded as
\emph{blocked}: source screening, licence pre-assessment, fetch attempts, and a
semantic-suitability assessment were carried out and three blocking factors
were documented. (i)~\textbf{Licence/network}: high-suitability sources were
excluded---US Census returned HTTP~403 (ToS conflict, permanently removed),
Wikidata timed out, \texttt{.gov.cn} sources were unreachable, and Wikipedia
body text was excluded for CC BY-SA copyleft. (ii)~\textbf{Semantic
suitability}: the only licence-clean downloadable candidate (SciTSR-pd, CC0)
is short scientific-table cell strings, not continuous factual sentences; it
cannot construct the F2/F3 slices and supports F1/F4/F5/F6 only partially, and
its original-file SHA-256 was never computed. (iii)~\textbf{Human/registration}:
the v2.5 cross-check protocol is frozen but has no applicable object while
\texttt{real=0}. Consequently no \texttt{cf\_pairs\_real.jsonl} was generated,
no real entries were written to the manifest, and no real run was started;
real-document external validity is \textbf{unverified}.

\paragraph{AI-generated data proposal: evaluated and withdrawn (not
activated).} The proposal to generate HellaSwag-seeded AI counterfactual data
(v2.6) was evaluated and \emph{withdrawn}: v2.6 remains frozen but
\emph{not activated}, no \texttt{data/generated/} directory was created, and no
human-seeded or model-generated dataset or result exists. We report no such
result. This is stated for completeness of the data-availability record only.

\paragraph{Residual limitations (disclosed, not fixed).} (i)~All data are
synthetic templates from a finite entity pool; even with 24 base structures the
corpus does not represent real-world distributions. (ii)~\texttt{real=0} with
M4 blocked: real-document external validity remains \emph{unverified}; we make
no claim of verified real-document performance or generalization across real
text. (iii)~The only clean-data evidence is the equal-depth MVP pair
(1.7B$\to$0.6B); cross-pair generalization is unevaluated. (iv)~61
length-mismatch pairs all fall in F5 (non-flip control) and are reported as a
separate stratum. (v)~Clean-data CIs are exploratory (8 families); the sealed
test was never evaluated.

\section{Method and Evaluation Protocol}
\label{sec:method}

\subsection{Setup and notation}
\label{sec:proposed}

A teacher $T$ prefill-computes a cache $C_T(d)$ of document $d$; a trainable
mapper $g_\theta$ maps it into receiver $S$'s cache format
$\hat{C}_S(d)=g_\theta(C_T(d))$. The receiver decodes query $q$ from
$\hat{C}_S(d)$ and never re-processes $d$. The receiver's own cache $C_S(d)$ is
an \emph{offline Oracle}: it may be used for training targets, evaluation
anchors, and intervention, but never inside the deployable inference path.
$S$'s main weights are frozen. The two documents of a counterfactual pair,
$d_A$ and $d_B$, are prefilled \emph{independently}; cache bytes must never be
tampered with to synthesize the other document. In the clean-data results below
the mapping arm is a zero-trainable-parameter affine mapper (closed-form ridge
initialization).

\subsection{Strict zero re-prefill and the arm boundary}
\label{sec:zerore}

We distinguish four arms by the provenance of Key and Value (Table~\ref{tab:arms}).
Only \emph{Joint} is deployable under strict zero re-prefill: the student
processes no document token and does not read $C_S(d)$ at inference. A forward
hook asserts that no document token enters the student and writes a per-run
\texttt{zero\_re\_prefill} flag. K-only, V-only, Self, Identity, and Oracle are
offline diagnostics or anchors and are not deployable methods.

\subsection{CF-RCT: proposed design only, never validated}
\label{sec:cf-rct}

We outline \texttt{CF-RCT} as a \emph{proposed design only}. It was never
trained or evaluated: with the central premise (a clean, utility-preserving
gap) withdrawn and P3 permanently cancelled, the method layer has no target.
The mapper is a per-(layer, channel$\in$\{K,V\}, head) affine map with an
optional low-rank residual $W=W_0+AB^\top$; non-equal-depth pairs use a
proportional layer map. Initialization is a closed-form ridge from teacher to
student caches on a calibration set; computation is in de-RoPE'd content space,
re-RoPE'd before injection, with bf16 + sdpa throughout. The intended objective
is $L=\lambda_{\mathrm{KD}}L_{\mathrm{KD}}+\lambda_{\mathrm{CF}}L_{\mathrm{CF}}
+\lambda_{\mathrm{local}}L_{\mathrm{local}}+\lambda_{\mathrm{reg}}L_{\mathrm{reg}}$.
We report no result for this design; contribution~(3) remains withheld. The
Huber threshold $\delta=0.5$ was registered under the frozen loss configuration,
but the $\delta$ verification recheck is permanently cancelled (the method route
is abandoned); no $\delta$-dependent result is reported.

\subsection{Metrics: $E_{\mathrm{CF}}$ and joint reporting}
\label{sec:metrics}

For a candidate pair, define $g(C,q)=\log P(A\mid C,q)-\log P(B\mid C,q)$ at a
single shared generation position. Then
\begin{align}
D_{\mathrm{self}} &= g(C_S(d_A),q)-g(C_S(d_B),q),\\
D_{\mathrm{trans}} &= g(\hat C_S(d_A),q)-g(\hat C_S(d_B),q),\\
E_{\mathrm{CF}} &= |D_{\mathrm{trans}}-D_{\mathrm{self}}|,\qquad
\mathrm{normalized}\ E_{\mathrm{CF}}=\frac{|D_{\mathrm{trans}}-D_{\mathrm{self}}|}
{|D_{\mathrm{self}}|+\tau},\qquad \tau=1.0 .
\end{align}
$E_{\mathrm{CF}}$ is not a quality measure by itself: when
$D_{\mathrm{self}}\approx0$, a constant output can achieve small
$E_{\mathrm{CF}}$. It is therefore always jointly reported with dual-side
per-document success \texttt{dual\_correct}, direction consistency
\texttt{flip\_consistency} on the Self-valid subset $|D_{\mathrm{self}}|\ge0.1$,
coverage \texttt{coverage}, and the gap to no-information controls
\texttt{info\_attribution} (control-group $E_{\mathrm{CF}}$ minus Joint
$E_{\mathrm{CF}}$). Because the control groups are separate arms, the per-pair
\texttt{info\_gap} field is unset and the aggregate \texttt{info\_attribution}
is reported. A high $E_{\mathrm{CF}}$ with collapsed dual correctness and
non-dominated controls is classified as broken transfer, \emph{not} as a
faithful-but-distorted response: the orthogonal \texttt{transfer\_broken}
annotation captures this.

\subsection{Primary endpoint and statistical discipline}
\label{sec:stats}

The primary endpoint is the paired $E_{\mathrm{CF}}$ difference on the sealed
test between a new method and the strongest behavioral baseline
(\texttt{primary\_baseline=kd\_only}), with a template-family-cluster paired
bootstrap (10{,}000 resamples, 95\% CI) and a pair-level sensitivity CI. A gain
would require the CI upper bound $<0$ with no joint-metric degradation; $\geq3$
seeds and Holm correction are required. This endpoint is \emph{not evaluated}:
no method was trained and the sealed test was never run. $P>0.05$ is nowhere
treated as evidence of equivalence. The frozen machine verdict
\texttt{distortion\_exists} remains in the artifacts but must be read jointly
with \texttt{transfer\_broken}; the two answer different questions
(Section~\ref{sec:kdbase}). Every table reporting $E_{\mathrm{CF}}$ below gives
the \texttt{template\_family} cluster-paired CI as the primary CI
($n_{\mathrm{clusters}}=8$, \texttt{exploratory=true}) and the pair-level CI as
an explicitly labelled sensitivity, never the reverse.

\subsection{Model pairs and baselines}
\label{sec:baselines}

Two model pairs were used in earlier work: (a)~\emph{MVP}, equal-depth
Qwen3-1.7B (teacher) $\to$ Qwen3-0.6B (student), $28\to28$ identity layer map;
and (b)~\emph{main validation}, non-equal-depth Qwen3-4B $\to$ Qwen3-1.7B,
$36\to28$ proportional layer map. All runs use device CUDA, dtype
\texttt{bfloat16}, kernel \texttt{sdpa}. Clean-data results reported here
(\texttt{p1\_clean\_mvp\_*} and \texttt{e0\_clean\_mvp\_*}) are at commit
\texttt{efc3ba3}; the clean \texttt{kd\_only} baseline is at \texttt{6413ba0}.
The design matches the calibration pairs, dtype/kernel, and forward budget
across arms. It does \emph{not} match trainable parameter count, training steps,
or tuning budget: in the clean-data results the mapping arm is a
zero-trainable-parameter affine mapper (closed-form ridge initialization,
$0$ trainable parameters, $0$ training epochs), whereas the \texttt{kd\_only}
control has $7{,}340{,}032$ trainable parameters trained for $8$ epochs. This
budget asymmetry is a limitation, disclosed in Section~\ref{sec:limitations};
the \texttt{kd\_only} control is reported in Section~\ref{sec:kdbase}.

\subsection{E0 implementation gate on clean data (P0)}
\label{sec:e0}

The implementation gate was re-verified on the cleaned corpus (run

\subsection{Adverse results and boundaries}
\label{sec:adverse}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Quality collapse dominates.} On clean data Joint
      \texttt{dual\_correct} collapses to $0.283$--$0.417$ from Self $\approx0.82$,
      and Joint $E_{\mathrm{CF}}$ approaches but does not exceed the
      mismatched/zero-cache controls. The broken-transfer label rests on the
      dual collapse, not on reaching the controls.
\item \textbf{Single-signal trigger and mixed controls.} \texttt{transfer\_broken}
      is triggered by a \emph{single signal} (the dual drop $\geq0.2$ on the
      Joint arm); seed0 information attributions are mixed (two positive, one
      negative). The failure classification rests on this joint pattern and is
      reported as a boundary observation, not a causal claim.
\item \textbf{Exploratory strength boundary.} All clean CIs are
      \texttt{exploratory} under v2.3 (8 \texttt{template\_family} clusters
      $<24$; wide CIs), come from a single synthetic equal-depth pair, and come
      from the validation split only---the sealed test was \emph{never
      evaluated}. We make no cross-pair, cross-model, real-world, causal, or
      strong-significance claim.
\item \textbf{Prior positive signals were data artifacts.} The reversal in
      Section~\ref{sec:reversal} means the previously reported gap and mechanism
      are not reproducible once the data are correct.
\item \textbf{Unverified external validity.} Synthetic-only data, \texttt{real=0}
      with M4 blocked, a limited SQuAD anchor (not replicated for kd), and the
      withdrawn AI-data proposal all bound external validity; no
      real-document or generated-data result is claimed.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{What the study establishes.} The evidence supports a narrow,
negative claim: with the data defects repaired, the tested affine mapper does
not transfer usefully on the single equal-depth pair, and the earlier positive
gap and mechanism were artifacts of the defective corpus. Because the
\texttt{template\_family} primary CIs have lower bounds above zero on all three
seeds, the failure direction is internally consistent; because they are
exploratory (8 clusters) and paired with wider intervals than the pair-level
sensitivity CIs, they do not license strong inference. We deliberately report
both CI sets for every $E_{\mathrm{CF}}$ table so that the narrower pair-level
interval cannot be mistaken for the confirmatory one (a known failure mode of
the pre-repair analysis).

\paragraph{Why this is not a method or mechanism paper.} The kd-only baseline
retains a nonzero $E_{\mathrm{CF}}$ while preserving dual correctness; under
the frozen scenario-C ruling this is an exploratory observation, not a restored
contribution. With the mechanism object absent, P2 never rerun, P3 permanently
cancelled, and the sealed-test endpoint unevaluated, no contribution of the
method or mechanism layer is available. The frozen machine verdict
\texttt{distortion\_exists} and the orthogonal \texttt{transfer\_broken=true}
answer different questions; we disclose both rather than reading the former as
a clean distortion.

\paragraph{Relation to prior work and novelty boundary.} The general
utility-versus-fidelity distinction, substitute-cache negative results, strong
cross-model transfer baselines, and cache-audit control methodology are all
anticipated in recent work~\citep{n1,n2,n3,n4,n5,n6,n7,n8,n9a}. We do not claim
any of them. The narrow novelty we can defend is a concrete preregistered
before/after reversal case with a paired counterfactual $E_{\mathrm{CF}}$ audit
trail on one synthetic benchmark. General leakage lessons belong to
\citet{kapoor2023}; paired counterfactual construction belongs to
\citet{contrastsets} and \citet{kaushik2020}. The SQuAD anchor is an
evaluation-only standard-QA probe~\citep{squad16,squad18}.

\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Single pair, synthetic domain.} The only clean-data evidence is
      one equal-depth MVP pair (1.7B$\to$0.6B) on synthetic templates; no
      cross-pair or cross-model claim is made. The non-equal-depth main pair
      appears only in superseded pre-fix artifacts.
\item \texttt{\textbf{real=0}.} Real-document collection (M4) was blocked by
      licence/network, semantic-suitability, and human/registration factors;
      no real split, manifest entry, or run exists, so real-document external
      validity is unverified. The v2.5 cross-check protocol has no applicable
      object under \texttt{real=0}.
\item \textbf{Exploratory statistics.} All clean CIs use
      \texttt{template\_family} as the primary cluster with $n_{\mathrm{clusters}}=8<24$
      and are flagged \texttt{exploratory}; the pair-level CIs are reported only
      as a sensitivity. The sealed test was never evaluated, so no
      confirmatory endpoint exists. $P>0.05$ is not treated as equivalence.
\item \textbf{Asymmetric training budget.} The affine mapper has $0$ trainable
      parameters and $0$ training epochs (closed-form ridge), whereas the
      \texttt{kd\_only} baseline has $7{,}340{,}032$ trainable parameters and
      $8$ training epochs. Calibration pairs, dtype/kernel, and forward budget
      are matched, but trainable parameter count, training steps, and tuning
      budget are \emph{not}. The affine-vs-kd comparison is therefore not a
      compute- or capacity-matched one, and cannot isolate a method effect.
\item \textbf{Single-signal failure label.} \texttt{transfer\_broken} is set by
      the Joint-arm dual drop alone; seed0 information attributions are mixed
      (two positive, one negative). The failure label is a boundary
      observation, not a causal mechanism claim.
\item \textbf{E_CF is not quality.} A large $E_{\mathrm{CF}}$ can coexist with
      collapsed answer correctness; we always report it jointly with dual
      success, direction consistency, coverage, and control-group gaps. Joint
      $E_{\mathrm{CF}}$ approaches but does not exceed the mismatch and
      zero-cache controls.
\item \textbf{Limited anchor.} SQuAD v2.0 dev is evaluation-only, single seed,
      240 answerable examples, EM at floor; it replicates the affine direction
      but not the kd utility-preserving contrast, and yields
      \texttt{exploratory\_disclosure}. It is not pooled with the synthetic
      test. ARC was not run.
\item \textbf{Withdrawn/withheld work.} No AI-generated data were produced
      (v2.6 not activated; no \texttt{data/generated/}); G1 strong-baseline
      confirmation was not performed; P2/P3/P4 are permanently cancelled. No
      result for any of these is reported or implied. Mock and smoke outputs
      are not scientific evidence.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We set out to measure counterfactual functional fidelity in cross-model KV
handoff. The earlier positive findings are withdrawn: they were produced by a
synthetic corpus with degenerate pairs, cross-split document leakage,
template-skeleton overlap, and a degenerate bootstrap. After rebuilding the
corpus with those defects removed, the apparent utility-preserving gap reverses
into a \emph{transfer failure}: on the single equal-depth MVP pair the tested
affine mapper collapses \texttt{dual\_correct} to $0.28$--$0.42$ (from Self
$\approx0.82$) while Joint $E_{\mathrm{CF}}$ rises to $6.90$--$8.54$,
approaching but not exceeding the no-information controls, with
\texttt{transfer\_broken=true} on all three seeds and primary/exploratory CIs
reported alongside pair-level sensitivity.

The paper is a \textbf{negative / empirical boundary and data-audit study} with
no positive contribution. It reports an affine transfer failure boundary under
the present data construction, a methodological negative result (concrete
data-quality defects manufactured a statistically convincing but false
positive, and repairing them erased and reversed it), and an exploratory
observation that the strong \texttt{kd\_only} baseline preserves transfer
utility while retaining a stable nonzero $E_{\mathrm{CF}}$ ($6.39$--$7.21$).
The SQuAD anchor yields \texttt{exploratory\_disclosure}, not a strengthening
of external validity.

We make \emph{no} claim of a general counterfactual gap, a receiver-side
mechanism, a general utility/fidelity distinction, a mechanism-layer first, or
any method improvement; nor do we claim a new audit method or general leakage
lesson. Contribution~(3) remains withheld; the primary endpoint (paired
$E_{\mathrm{CF}}$ vs.\ \texttt{kd\_only}, CI upper $<0$) is unevaluated; P2/P3/P4
are \emph{permanently cancelled}; the sealed test was never evaluated; G1 was
not performed; and the v2.6 AI-data proposal is withdrawn and not activated.
All clean-data conclusions are \texttt{exploratory} (8 \texttt{template\_family}
clusters $<24$), come from a single synthetic equal-depth pair, and rest on the
single dual-drop signal for the broken-transfer label. These limits bound every
conclusion above.
