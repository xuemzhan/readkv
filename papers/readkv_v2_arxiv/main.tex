\begin{abstract}
Cross-model key--value (KV) cache transfer promises zero re-prefill of a
source document. We studied a paired synthetic benchmark where an earlier
release reported an apparent counterfactual gap and a receiver-side mechanism.
A data audit found 18 degenerate pairs, verbatim cross-split leakage, template
overlap, and a pair-level i.i.d.\ bootstrap. In a controlled version contrast
the finding did not survive corpus reconstruction: on one synthetic equal-depth
pair (1.7B$\to$0.6B) the affine Joint arm's dual correctness falls from
$\approx0.82$ to $0.28$--$0.42$, with $E_{\mathrm{CF}}$ $6.90$--$8.54$
approaching but \emph{not} exceeding mismatched ($8.38$) and zero-cache
($8.58$) controls; a preregistered 2$\times$2 contrast (12 cells) shows the
Self-minus-Joint gap rising in all nine reconstruction cells at the
point-estimate level. \texttt{transfer\_broken} follows a single dual-drop
signal. A \texttt{kd\_only} baseline on the same pairs/config shows a smaller
aggregate dual drop ($11.7$/$16.7$/$1.7$ points), sensitive to slice
definition, reported only as exploratory. All clean CIs are exploratory
(8 \texttt{template\_family} clusters, $<24$); the sealed test was never
evaluated. We withdraw contributions~(1) and~(2) and withhold~(3): the old
mechanism evidence is withdrawn, but whether a mechanism exists on clean data
is undecided (no clean P2). No method was trained or improvement claimed.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Cross-model KV handoff maps a teacher's document cache into a receiver
student's cache format so that the student can answer queries about a document
without re-processing it (\emph{strict zero re-prefill}). An earlier version of
this study asked whether the student's \emph{functional dependence on the
document} survives transfer, formalized as counterfactual functional fidelity
and measured by the counterfactual error $E_{\mathrm{CF}}$
(Section~\ref{sec:metrics}). It reported two positive findings on a synthetic
corpus: an apparent gap between ordinary transfer utility and counterfactual
fidelity, and a receiver-side Value-consumption mechanism. \textbf{Both are
withdrawn here.}

The paper is therefore not a method or mechanism paper. Its subject is the
\emph{audit trail} around those findings and the boundary condition observed
once the data were repaired. An independent data-quality audit found that the
original corpus contained degenerate pairs (identical candidates marked
must-flip), cross-split verbatim document leakage, and train/test
template-skeleton overlap, and that its cluster bootstrap had degenerated to
pair-level i.i.d. In a controlled contrast between corpus versions (degenerate-
pair removal, split de-duplication, and structural template changes applied
together), the reported gap did not survive: on the reconstructed corpus the
previously ``utility-preserving gap'' became a \emph{transfer failure}
(Section~\ref{sec:reversal}). This version contrast identifies only the whole
corpus-version switch, not any single defect (Section~\ref{sec:audit2x2}).
The old mechanism \emph{evidence} is withdrawn; whether a mechanism exists on
clean data is \emph{undecided}, because no clean-data mechanism experiment was
run. This is consistent with the general reproducibility warning of
\citet{kapoor2023}, but our contribution is the concrete, preregistered
version contrast and the paired counterfactual $E_{\mathrm{CF}}$ audit trail
in this specific benchmark, not the general lesson itself.

\paragraph{Findings reported here (negative / empirical results, not positive
contributions).}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Failure boundary.} On clean data the tested affine/ridge mapper
      fails to transfer usefully on the single equal-depth MVP pair: dual-side
      correctness collapses to $0.28$--$0.42$ from a native $\approx0.78$--$0.90$,
      and $E_{\mathrm{CF}}$ rises towards but does not reach the
      mismatched/zero-cache controls (Section~\ref{sec:mvp}). This is a
      boundary condition under the present data construction, not a general
      property of KV transfer.
\item \textbf{Exploratory gap on the strong baseline.} The \texttt{kd\_only}
      baseline on the same clean pair has a smaller aggregate dual-accuracy
      drop from Self ($\approx11.7$/$16.7$/$1.7$ points across seeds;
      \texttt{transfer\_broken=false}), yet this drop is sensitive to slice
      definition (F1--F4 seed 1 reaches $21.7$ points) and a nonzero
      $E_{\mathrm{CF}}$ of $6.39$--$7.21$ remains. We report this \emph{only as
      an exploratory observation} on one synthetic pair, and do \emph{not}
      claim established utility preservation or a restored contribution
      (Section~\ref{sec:kdbase}).
\item \textbf{Methodological negative result.} A controlled contrast between
      two corpus versions (version changes applied together) shows that the
      reported finding did not survive reconstruction of the benchmark corpus;
      the contrast identifies only the whole version switch, not any single
      defect (Sections~\ref{sec:reversal},~\ref{sec:audit2x2}).
\item \textbf{Limited external anchor.} On a SQuAD~v2.0 dev single-reference
      subset, both transferred arms show a descriptive F1 decrease (affine to
      $2.6\%$ of Self, kd to $0.7\%$) and all arms hit an EM floor of
      undiagnosed cause; under the preregistered v2.4 rule this is
      \texttt{exploratory\_disclosure}, not a strengthening of external
      validity (Section~\ref{sec:anchor}).
\end{enumerate}
None of these is a positive contribution in the sense of the frozen
contribution criteria. We no longer claim to identify a general counterfactual
gap, to localize a mechanism, or to improve transfer. Contribution~(3) remains
\emph{withheld}, the primary endpoint is unevaluated, P2/P3/P4 are permanently
cancelled, and real-document external validity is unverified (\texttt{real=0}).
The manuscript is ready-to-submit at TMLR, pending a new sign-off from the real
PI over the revised text; final submission is made by the real PI in person
(Section~\ref{sec:status}).

\section{Background and Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Cross-model KV transfer and the utility/fidelity question.}
Behavioral distillation of per-layer linear K/V maps
(\citep{kvlingo}) and functional cache transport with frozen-receiver
supervision (\citep{cachelink}) take ordinary task utility or token-distribution
matching as their stated target. Their reported abstracts state that
reconstruction fidelity need not coincide with receiver utility and that a
closed-form ridge mapping can degrade sharply on some model
pairs~\citep{heo2026}; we have verified these works at abstract level only and
make no claim about section-level diagnostic detail. These works score ordinary
task quality, not paired counterfactual response change; we use them as
baselines and conceptual precursors, and note only that our ``partial-pair
transfer failure'' is compatible with the degradation described in the Heo
et al.\ abstract. We do not treat the utility/fidelity distinction as our
invention.

\paragraph{A crowded 2026 method and audit landscape.}
As reported in their abstracts, recent work already separates utility from
fidelity: a cross-version study names \emph{Functional Cache Divergence} and
reports a distinction between task utility and fidelity to native
behavior~\citep{n1}; a fidelity--consumability study reports that eight
substitute-cache methods remain at the no-information baseline and that
``more faithful'' need not mean ``consumable''~\citep{n2}; a safety study finds
value-reuse bias exceeds key-reuse bias and applies per-layer/per-head affine
correction~\citep{n3}. Strong transfer baselines and failure analyses---sparse
query-relevant transfer~\citep{n4}, prefill-free cross-family
transfer~\citep{n5}, rank-disagreement selective
recomputation~\citep{n6}, and training-free cache
fusion~\citep{n8}---further compress the methodological space, and hybrid/linear
attention translation is also being explored~\citep{n9a,n9b}. A causal audit of
relayed KV caches uses mismatched/zero/random cache controls with a
preregistered equivalence test~\citep{n7}, anticipating our control-group and
information-attribution discipline. These works anticipate both the general
utility-versus-fidelity distinction and the control-group methodology; our
contribution is \emph{not} a general distinction, a mechanism-layer first, or a
new audit method, but a specific preregistered reversal case with a paired
counterfactual $E_{\mathrm{CF}}$ audit trail. Our characterizations of this
2026 preprint literature are abstract-level; full-text verification was not
completed.

\paragraph{Counterfactual and faithfulness evaluation.}
Paired minimal-pair construction that changes the gold label is established by
Contrast Sets~\citep{contrastsets} and counterfactually augmented
data~\citep{kaushik2020}; $E_{\mathrm{CF}}$ is a continuous logit-difference
adaptation of that idea to the transfer setting, not a new evaluation paradigm.
Under KV-cache compression, ``Does Accuracy Equal Evidence?''~\citep{acc_evidence}
shows that answer accuracy can be preserved while evidence faithfulness degrades;
we distinguish their within-model, compression, reasoning-chain setting from
our cross-model, paired counterfactual-document setting. Real-document external
validity is not claimed: the AI-generated-data proposal was evaluated and
withdrawn (not activated; see Section~\ref{sec:datafix}). The only standard-QA
anchor we report is SQuAD~v2.0~\citep{squad16,squad18}, used for evaluation
only (Section~\ref{sec:anchor}).

\section{Research Questions}
\label{sec:rq}

The study is framed by four descriptive, negative/empirical questions. They
carry no positive-contribution threshold and are not method-paper claims.

\begin{description}
\item[RQ1 (methodological lesson; core).] Did the reported
      ``utility-preserving gap'' survive reconstruction of the benchmark
      corpus? Evidence: the defect inventory and repair report
      (Section~\ref{sec:datafix}), the before/after version contrast
      (Section~\ref{sec:reversal}), and the controlled 2$\times$2 contrast
      (Section~\ref{sec:audit2x2}). The whole-version switch is identified; no
      single defect is attributed as the cause.
\item[RQ2 (failure boundary; core).] On clean data, under which conditions does
      the tested affine mapper fail to transfer usefully? Evidence: three clean
      seeds on the equal-depth pair, dual-side collapse, and the
      \texttt{transfer\_broken} annotation read jointly with the frozen machine
      verdict (Sections~\ref{sec:mvp},~\ref{sec:discussion}). Adjustment: we
      disclose that $E_{\mathrm{CF}}$ approaches but does not exceed the
      mismatch/zero-cache controls and that the break flag rests on one signal.
\item[RQ3 (exploratory observation; not a contribution).] Does the strong
      \texttt{kd\_only} baseline have a smaller dual-accuracy drop while
      retaining a nonzero $E_{\mathrm{CF}}$, and is that drop stable to slice
      definition? Evidence: three clean kd seeds and the SQuAD anchor
      (Sections~\ref{sec:kdbase},~\ref{sec:anchor}). Adjustment: reported as an
      exploratory observation only; not a restoration of any withdrawn claim,
      and not established utility preservation.
\item[RQ4 (external-validity and robustness).] With \texttt{real=0}, synthetic
      data, and a single pair, how far do compliant anchors and a preregistered
      scoring-robustness check extend the conclusions? Evidence: the
      evaluation-only SQuAD anchor, the blocked real-document attempt, and the
      sequence-scoring check (Sections~\ref{sec:datafix},~\ref{sec:anchor},
      \ref{sec:seqscore}). Adjustment: \texttt{real=0} and the single synthetic
      equal-depth pair are disclosed as limitations; the scoring check reports
      only point-estimate direction consistency.
\end{description}

\section{Data and Audit}
\label{sec:dataaudit}

\subsection{Counterfactual data construction: slices F1--F6 and denominators}
\label{sec:data}

Each evaluation unit is $u=(q,d_A,d_B,a,b,m)$: a query and a pair of documents
differing in one targeted fact, with candidates A/B held fixed within the pair
and their order randomly swapped. Six slices exclude distinct confounds:
F1 direct fact substitution; F2 reasoning-chain change; F3 relation/negation
reversal; F4 distractors and coreference; F5 surface equivalence (a
\emph{non-flip negative control}); F6 length/position control. The rebuilt
corpus has 520 pairs (probe 40 / train 120 / val 60 / test 300 / \texttt{real 0}).

\paragraph{Three distinct denominators.} The number $398$ is the
\emph{corpus-level} count of must-flip pairs after excluding F5 and F6; it is
not the denominator of any per-run metric. Each validation cell contains 60
pairs, of which 46 belong to F1--F4 (the only slices with a must-flip gold
label used here). The per-pair flip metric is further restricted to the
Self-valid subset $|D_{\mathrm{self}}|\ge0.1$. For the reconstructed affine
cells this yields, after the F1--F4 and Self-threshold filters, $34/45$,
$35/46$, and $34/46$ evaluable must-flip pairs for seeds 0/1/2 (frozen
per-pair logs; first-round review). We report these slice-level denominators
alongside the all-slice algorithm that produced the frozen summary tables, and
treat the latter \texttt{exploratory}.

\paragraph{First-token proxy and F5/F6 behaviour.} The score $g$ in
Section~\ref{sec:metrics} is a \emph{binary proxy}: it uses the log-probability
of the candidate \emph{first token} at one shared generation position, not a
full-sequence likelihood. Under this readout, F5 pairs have identical candidate
texts, so the proxy error is exactly zero and dual-side correctness is $1$ for
F5; F5 therefore has no discriminative power for this metric and is excluded
from must-flip denominators. F6 changes the person name \emph{and} the gold
label (the answer still swaps between A and B); it is a length/position control
and is likewise excluded from the frozen flip denominator. Full candidate-text
(candidate-sequence) scoring is evaluated as a preregistered robustness check
in Section~\ref{sec:seqscore}.

\section{Method and Evaluation Protocol}
\label{sec:method}

\subsection{Setup, notation, and arm boundary}
\label{sec:proposed}

A teacher $T$ prefill-computes a cache $C_T(d)$ of document $d$; a mapper
$g_\theta$ maps it into receiver $S$'s cache format
$\hat{C}_S(d)=g_\theta(C_T(d))$. The receiver decodes query $q$ from
$\hat{C}_S(d)$ and never re-processes $d$. The receiver's own cache $C_S(d)$ is
an \emph{offline Oracle}: it may be used for training targets, evaluation
anchors, and intervention, but never inside the deployable inference path.
$S$'s main weights are frozen. The two documents of a counterfactual pair,
$d_A$ and $d_B$, are prefilled \emph{independently}; cache bytes must never be
tampered with to synthesize the other document.

In the clean-data results the mapping arm is a closed-form ridge affine map
with \emph{zero gradient-optimization epochs}: it estimates affine coefficients
from calibration data but has no gradient-trained parameters (roughly $7.4$M
matrix and intercept coefficients, first-round review). We distinguish four
arms by the provenance of Key and Value (Table~\ref{tab:arms}). Only \emph{Joint}
is deployable under strict zero re-prefill: the student processes no document
token and does not read $C_S(d)$ at inference. A forward hook asserts that no
document token enters the student and writes a per-run
\texttt{zero\_re\_prefill} flag. K-only, V-only, Self, Identity, and Oracle are
offline diagnostics or anchors and are not deployable methods.

\subsection{CF-RCT: proposed design only, never validated}
\label{sec:cf-rct}

We outline \texttt{CF-RCT} as a \emph{proposed design only}. It was never
trained or evaluated: with the central premise (a clean, utility-preserving
gap) withdrawn and P3 permanently cancelled, the method layer has no target.
The mapper is a per-(layer, channel$\in$\{K,V\}, head) affine map with an
optional low-rank residual $W=W_0+AB^\top$; non-equal-depth pairs use a
proportional layer map. Initialization is a closed-form ridge from teacher to
student caches on a calibration set; computation is in de-RoPE'd content space,
re-RoPE'd before injection, with bf16 + sdpa throughout. The intended objective
is $L=\lambda_{\mathrm{KD}}L_{\mathrm{KD}}+\lambda_{\mathrm{CF}}L_{\mathrm{CF}}
+\lambda_{\mathrm{local}}L_{\mathrm{local}}+\lambda_{\mathrm{reg}}L_{\mathrm{reg}}$.
We report no result for this design; contribution~(3) remains withheld. The
Huber threshold $\delta=0.5$ was registered under the frozen loss configuration,
but the $\delta$ verification recheck is permanently cancelled (the method route
is abandoned); no $\delta$-dependent result is reported.

\subsection{Metrics: $E_{\mathrm{CF}}$ and joint reporting}
\label{sec:metrics}

For a candidate pair, define $g(C,q)=\log P(A\mid C,q)-\log P(B\mid C,q)$ at a
single shared generation position, where $P(A\mid C,q)$ and $P(B\mid C,q)$ are
the probabilities of the \emph{candidate first tokens} A and B
(Section~\ref{sec:data}). Then
\begin{align}
D_{\mathrm{self}} &= g(C_S(d_A),q)-g(C_S(d_B),q),\\
D_{\mathrm{trans}} &= g(\hat C_S(d_A),q)-g(\hat C_S(d_B),q),\\
E_{\mathrm{CF}} &= |D_{\mathrm{trans}}-D_{\mathrm{self}}|,\qquad
\mathrm{normalized}\ E_{\mathrm{CF}}=\frac{|D_{\mathrm{trans}}-D_{\mathrm{self}}|}
{|D_{\mathrm{self}}|+\tau},\qquad \tau=1.0 .
\end{align}
$E_{\mathrm{CF}}$ is a non-negative absolute difference at the first-token
level and equals zero for Self and Identity \emph{by definition}; the
template-family interval excluding zero describes error magnitude but cannot
by itself establish a practically meaningful utility--fidelity separation.
$E_{\mathrm{CF}}$ is not a quality measure by itself: when
$D_{\mathrm{self}}\approx0$, a constant output can achieve small
$E_{\mathrm{CF}}$. It is therefore always jointly reported with dual-side
per-document success \texttt{dual\_correct}, direction consistency
\texttt{flip\_consistency} on the Self-valid subset $|D_{\mathrm{self}}|\ge0.1$,
coverage \texttt{coverage}, and the gap to no-information controls
\texttt{info\_attribution} (control-group $E_{\mathrm{CF}}$ minus Joint
$E_{\mathrm{CF}}$). Because the control groups are separate arms, the per-pair
\texttt{info\_gap} field is unset and the aggregate \texttt{info\_attribution}
is reported. A high $E_{\mathrm{CF}}$ with collapsed dual correctness and
non-dominated controls is classified as broken transfer, \emph{not} as a
faithful-but-distorted response: the orthogonal \texttt{transfer\_broken}
annotation captures this.

\subsection{Primary endpoint, statistical discipline, and fair alignment}
\label{sec:stats}

The primary endpoint is the paired $E_{\mathrm{CF}}$ difference on the sealed
test between a new method and the strongest behavioral baseline
(\texttt{primary\_baseline=kd\_only}), with a template-family-cluster paired
bootstrap (10{,}000 resamples, 95\% CI) and a pair-level sensitivity CI. A gain
would require the CI upper bound $<0$ with no joint-metric degradation; $\geq3$
seeds and Holm correction are required. This endpoint is \emph{not evaluated}:
no method was trained and the sealed test was never run. $P>0.05$ is nowhere
treated as evidence of equivalence. The frozen machine verdict
\texttt{distortion\_exists} remains in the artifacts but must be read jointly
with \texttt{transfer\_broken}; the two answer different questions
(Section~\ref{sec:kdbase}). Every table reporting $E_{\mathrm{CF}}$ below gives
the \texttt{template\_family} cluster-paired CI as the primary CI
($n_{\mathrm{clusters}}=8$, \texttt{exploratory=true}) and the pair-level CI as
an explicitly labelled sensitivity, never the reverse.

The arms are aligned on the counterfactual pairs, dtype/kernel, and forward
budget across \texttt{affine}, \texttt{consumer}, \texttt{kd\_only},
\texttt{score}, ridge, CE-only, contrastive, and mismatched-document controls.
We do \emph{not} claim alignment on trainable parameter count, training steps,
or tuning budget: these budgets are deliberately unequal between the two arms
we compare on clean data. The affine map has zero gradient-optimization epochs
(closed-form ridge), whereas the \texttt{kd\_only} baseline has $7{,}340{,}032$
gradient-optimised parameters trained for $8$ epochs (frozen
\texttt{p1\_kd\_clean\_mvp\_seed\{0,1,2\}/metrics.json}). The clean-data
comparison is therefore not a parameter- or step-matched ablation; this
asymmetry is disclosed as a limitation (Section~\ref{sec:limitations}). Two
model pairs were used in earlier work: equal-depth Qwen3-1.7B$\to$Qwen3-0.6B
(MVP, $28\to28$ identity layer map) and non-equal-depth Qwen3-4B$\to$Qwen3-1.7B
($36\to28$ proportional map). Runs use CUDA, \texttt{bfloat16}, \texttt{sdpa};
clean-data results are at commits \texttt{efc3ba3} (affine) and
\texttt{6413ba0} (\texttt{kd\_only}).

exploratory (8 clusters $<24$), so none of these intervals is a
strong-significance claim.

the second-round review with paired resampling within each fixed validation set,
independent template-family resampling of the two validation versions, and
sample weighting (10{,}000 resamples). The per-seed estimates are

for seeds 0/1/2: the point estimates have mixed sign and \emph{all three
exploratory intervals cover zero}. The defensible reading is that switching
either corpus version changes the point estimates, while the interaction
direction is not stable. Under the preregistered v2.7 rule this is
\texttt{mixed\_interaction} (not an upgrade to
\texttt{calibration\_version\_effect}).

effect of switching the \emph{whole} corpus version (calibration and/or
validation); it cannot attribute the change to de-duplication, degenerate-pair
removal, or template overlap individually, each of which would need its own
intervention (not performed). The structurally unseen templates added in the
rebuild reside mainly in probe/test and cannot be inferred from this validation
contrast. This boundary governs every causal statement in the paper: we report
a controlled version-sensitivity observation, not a single-defect cause. Full
cell-by-cell CIs, the predeclared contrasts, the three prespecified sensitivity
slices, and the non-paired resampling note for cross-version validation
comparisons are deferred to supplementary material; no additional numbers are
introduced here. Contribution~(1) and (2) remain withdrawn, contribution~(3)
withheld, and the primary endpoint unevaluated.

\subsection{Adverse results and boundaries}
\label{sec:adverse}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Quality collapse dominates.} On clean data Joint
      \texttt{dual\_correct} collapses to $0.283$--$0.417$ from Self $\approx0.82$,
      and Joint $E_{\mathrm{CF}}$ approaches but does not exceed the
      mismatched/zero-cache controls. The broken-transfer label rests on the
      dual collapse, not on reaching the controls.
\item \textbf{Single-signal trigger and mixed controls.} \texttt{transfer\_broken}
      is triggered by a \emph{single signal} (the dual drop $\geq0.2$ on the
      Joint arm); seed0 information attributions are mixed (two positive, one
      negative). The failure classification rests on this joint pattern and is
      reported as a boundary observation, not a causal claim.
\item \textbf{Exploratory strength boundary.} All clean CIs are
      \texttt{exploratory} under v2.3 (8 \texttt{template\_family} clusters
      $<24$; wide CIs), come from a single synthetic equal-depth pair, and come
      from the validation split only---the sealed test was \emph{never
      evaluated}. We make no cross-pair, cross-model, real-world, causal, or
      strong-significance claim.
\item \textbf{Prior positive signals did not survive reconstruction.} In the
      controlled version contrast of Section~\ref{sec:reversal}, the previously
      reported gap judgement reverses; because the corpus changes were applied
      together, we do not attribute this to a single defect. The old mechanism
      \emph{evidence} is withdrawn, while whether a mechanism exists on clean
      data is undecided (no clean P2 was run)---we do not claim it was shown
      absent.
\item \textbf{Unverified external validity.} Synthetic-only data, \texttt{real=0}
      with M4 blocked, a limited SQuAD anchor (not replicated for kd), and the
      withdrawn AI-data proposal all bound external validity; no
      real-document or generated-data result is claimed.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{What the study establishes.} The evidence supports a narrow,
negative claim: with the data defects repaired, the tested affine mapper does
not transfer usefully on the single equal-depth pair, and the earlier positive
gap did not survive reconstruction of the corpus. Because the
\texttt{template\_family} primary CIs have lower bounds above zero on all three
seeds, the failure direction is internally consistent; because they are
exploratory (8 clusters) and paired with wider intervals than the pair-level
sensitivity CIs, they do not license strong inference. We deliberately report
both CI sets for every $E_{\mathrm{CF}}$ table so that the narrower pair-level
interval cannot be mistaken for the confirmatory one (a known failure mode of
the pre-repair analysis).

\paragraph{Whole-version contrast, not single-defect attribution.} The 2$\times$2
contrast (Section~\ref{sec:audit2x2}) and the sequence-scoring robustness check
(Section~\ref{sec:seqscore}) both probe the version sensitivity without
attributing it to any single defect. In the 2$\times$2 design the interaction
direction is not stable and the relevant cross-version intervals cross zero in
3 of 6 cases; under the frozen v2.7 rule the verdict is
\texttt{mixed\_interaction}. In the scoring check, the preregistered branch is
\texttt{consistent\_across\_scoring}: the point estimates keep the same sign
under first-token and full-sequence scoring. Neither result is a
strong-significance, method-gain, or contribution-restoration claim; both are
exploratory (6--8 clusters $<24$).

\paragraph{Why this is not a method or mechanism paper.} The kd-only baseline
has a smaller dual-accuracy drop that is sensitive to slice definition but
retains a nonzero $E_{\mathrm{CF}}$; under the frozen scenario-C ruling this is
an exploratory observation, not a restored contribution. With the mechanism
object absent (old mechanism evidence withdrawn; clean-data existence undecided
because no clean P2 was run), P2 never rerun, P3 permanently cancelled, and the
sealed-test endpoint unevaluated, no contribution of the method or mechanism
layer is available. The frozen machine verdict \texttt{distortion\_exists} and
the orthogonal \texttt{transfer\_broken=true} answer different questions; we
disclose both rather than reading the former as a clean distortion.

\paragraph{Relation to prior work and novelty boundary.} The general
utility-versus-fidelity distinction, substitute-cache negative results, strong
cross-model transfer baselines, and cache-audit control methodology are all
anticipated in recent work~\citep{n1,n2,n3,n4,n5,n6,n7,n8,n9a}. We do not claim
any of them. The narrow novelty we can defend is a concrete preregistered
before/after version-contrast case with a paired counterfactual $E_{\mathrm{CF}}$
audit trail on one synthetic benchmark. General leakage lessons belong to
\citet{kapoor2023}; paired counterfactual construction belongs to
\citet{contrastsets} and \citet{kaushik2020}. The SQuAD anchor is an
evaluation-only standard-QA probe~\citep{squad16,squad18}.

\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Single pair, synthetic domain.} The only clean-data evidence is
      one equal-depth MVP pair (1.7B$\to$0.6B) on synthetic templates; no
      cross-pair or cross-model claim is made. The non-equal-depth main pair
      appears only in superseded pre-fix artifacts.
\item \texttt{\textbf{real=0}.} Real-document collection (M4) was blocked by
      licence/network, semantic-suitability, and human/registration factors;
      no real split, manifest entry, or run exists, so real-document external
      validity is unverified. The v2.5 cross-check protocol has no applicable
      object under \texttt{real=0}.
\item \textbf{Exploratory statistics.} All clean CIs use
      \texttt{template\_family} as the primary cluster with $n_{\mathrm{clusters}}=8<24$
      and are flagged \texttt{exploratory}; the pair-level CIs are reported only
      as a sensitivity. The 2$\times$2 and sequence-scoring analyses use 8 and
      6 (primary) families, respectively, all $<24$. The sealed test was never
      evaluated, so no confirmatory endpoint exists. $P>0.05$ is not treated as
      equivalence.
\item \textbf{Asymmetric training budget.} The affine map has zero
      gradient-optimization epochs (closed-form ridge, roughly $7.4$M estimated
      coefficients), whereas the \texttt{kd\_only} baseline has $7{,}340{,}032$
      gradient-optimised parameters and $8$ training epochs. Calibration pairs,
      dtype/kernel, and forward budget are matched, but trainable parameter
      count, training steps, and tuning budget are \emph{not}. The clean-data
      comparison is not a parameter- or step-matched ablation, and cannot
      isolate a method effect.
\item \textbf{Single-signal failure label.} \texttt{transfer\_broken} is set by
      the Joint-arm dual drop alone; seed0 information attributions are mixed
      (two positive, one negative). The failure label is a boundary
      observation, not a causal mechanism claim.
\item \textbf{$E_{\mathrm{CF}}$ is not quality.} A large $E_{\mathrm{CF}}$ can coexist with
      collapsed answer correctness; we always report it jointly with dual
      success, direction consistency, coverage, and control-group gaps. Joint
      $E_{\mathrm{CF}}$ approaches but does not exceed the mismatch and
      zero-cache controls, and is never compared across scoring readings.
\item \textbf{Limited anchor.} SQuAD v2.0 dev is evaluation-only; the evaluated
      subset is the first 240 answerable questions (two articles) scored against
      the first reference answer, single seed, EM at floor of undiagnosed cause;
      it replicates the affine direction but not the kd contrast, and yields
      \texttt{exploratory\_disclosure}. It is not pooled with the synthetic
      test. ARC was not run.
\item \textbf{Withdrawn/withheld work.} No AI-generated data were produced
      (v2.6 not activated; no \texttt{data/generated/}); G1 strong-baseline
      confirmation was not performed; P2/P3/P4 are permanently cancelled. No
      result for any of these is reported or implied. Mock and smoke outputs
      are not scientific evidence.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We set out to measure counterfactual functional fidelity in cross-model KV
handoff. The earlier positive findings are withdrawn: they were reported on a
synthetic corpus that an audit later found to contain degenerate pairs,
cross-split document leakage, template-skeleton overlap, and a degenerate
bootstrap. In a controlled contrast between the original and reconstructed
corpus versions (changes applied together; acceptance 11/11; v2.3 statistics),
the reported gap did not survive and the judgement reversed: on the single
equal-depth MVP pair the tested affine mapper collapses \texttt{dual\_correct}
to $0.28$--$0.42$ (from Self $\approx0.82$) while Joint $E_{\mathrm{CF}}$ rises
to $6.90$--$8.54$, approaching but not exceeding the no-information controls,
with \texttt{transfer\_broken=true} on all three seeds and primary/exploratory
CIs reported alongside pair-level sensitivity.

The paper is a \textbf{negative / empirical boundary and version-contrast
study} with no positive contribution. It reports an affine transfer failure
boundary under the present data construction on one equal-depth pair, a
methodological negative result (the reported finding did not survive a
controlled whole-version corpus contrast, not single-defect attribution), the
preregistered 2$\times$2 and sequence-scoring robustness observations (both
exploratory), and an exploratory observation that the strong \texttt{kd\_only}
baseline has a smaller aggregate dual-accuracy drop sensitive to slice
definition while retaining a stable nonzero $E_{\mathrm{CF}}$ ($6.39$--$7.21$).
The SQuAD anchor yields \texttt{exploratory\_disclosure}, not a strengthening
of external validity.

We make \emph{no} claim of a general counterfactual gap, a receiver-side
mechanism (the old mechanism evidence is withdrawn; its existence on clean data
is undecided because no clean P2 was run), a general utility/fidelity
distinction, a mechanism-layer first, any method improvement, a new audit
method, or a general leakage lesson. Contribution~(3) remains withheld; the
primary endpoint (paired $E_{\mathrm{CF}}$ vs.\ \texttt{kd\_only}, CI upper
$<0$) is unevaluated; P2/P3/P4 are \emph{permanently cancelled}; the sealed test
was never evaluated; G1 was not performed; and the v2.6 AI-data proposal is
withdrawn and not activated. All clean-data conclusions are \texttt{exploratory}
(8 \texttt{template\_family} clusters $<24$; 6 in the scoring primary analysis),
come from a single synthetic equal-depth pair, and rest on the single dual-drop
signal for the broken-transfer label. These limits bound every conclusion above.
